The Problem

Exams are stressful.
Planning shouldn't be.

Indian college students face massive syllabi with zero guidance on what to study, when to study it, or how much time each topic needs.

📚

Information Overload

7-8 subjects, hundreds of topics, notes scattered across PDFs, slides, and WhatsApp groups.

📅

No Smart Planning

Manual timetables and generic YouTube plans don't account for your strengths, weaknesses, or exam weightage.

😰

Last-Minute Cramming

Without a feedback loop on what you've actually retained, students only find out they're unprepared during the exam.

The Solution

An AI that reads your syllabus
and builds your study plan.

StudyPilot analyses your syllabus PDF and past exam papers, identifies high-weightage topics, assesses your knowledge level, and generates a personalised, adaptive study timetable — complete with flashcards, quizzes, and revision alerts.
